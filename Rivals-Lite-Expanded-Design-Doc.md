# Rivals Lite — June 10 Update

## A more tactical, more social version of Rivals Lite
Rivals Lite is getting a big step forward with new ways to fight, recover, and play with friends. The June 10 update introduces fresh multiplayer support, a weapon rework, a new knife playstyle, utility options, and a natural healing system that makes matches feel deeper and more rewarding.

This update is all about creating more meaningful fights, better team play, and a clearer sense of strategy in every match.

---

## Multiplayer is getting stronger
Rivals Lite now supports a more polished multiplayer experience with:
- Firebase auto-connect for faster joining
- Room-based play with shared codes
- 1v1 and 2v2 match options
- Party support so friends can play together more easily

Whether you want a quick duel or a full party match, the update makes it easier to jump in and play.

---

## Weapon rework
The weapon balance has been refreshed to make combat feel faster and more responsive.

### Primary Weapon
- Damage reduced from 50 to 20
- Projectile speed increased
- Reload time improved from 3 seconds to 1.5 seconds

### Secondary Weapon
- Damage adjusted to 7 per hit
- Built to feel fast and reliable in pressure situations

These changes make gunplay feel smoother while keeping the action intense.

---

## The Knife arrives
The knife adds a new high-risk, high-reward close-range option for players who like aggressive movement and smart positioning.

### Knife Stats
- 35 damage
- 3-second cooldown

### Backstab Bonus
- Attacking from behind an enemy deals 50 damage

This creates a new way to punish overcommitted opponents and turn a fight in an instant.

---

## Better movement, better maps
Players can no longer walk through walls, making movement and positioning more reliable.

That means fights feel cleaner, maps feel more intentional, and players have to think more carefully about cover and angles.

---

## New utility options
The June 10 update introduces a new utility system that adds more ways to control the pace of a fight.

### Grenade
- 50 damage
- 25-second cooldown
- Shows the landing area before impact
- Great for pressure, pushing enemies, and controlling space

### Medkit
- 70 HP heal
- 5-second use time
- 30-second cooldown

These tools give players more tactical choices beyond direct gunplay.

---

## Natural healing
Rivals Lite now includes a natural healing system that rewards patience and smart positioning.

### How it works
- After taking damage, the player must wait 5 seconds without taking more damage
- Once the delay ends, healing begins
- The player heals 2 HP per second
- Taking damage resets the timer

This creates a more forgiving survival loop while still rewarding strong defensive play.

---

## Loadouts made simple
Every player always starts with:
- Primary weapon
- Secondary weapon
- Melee weapon

Then they choose one utility:
- Medkit
- Grenade

This keeps the loadout system easy to understand while still giving players meaningful choices.

---

## The future of Rivals Lite
The June 10 update pushes Rivals Lite toward a more tactical, team-friendly, and action-driven experience. With stronger multiplayer support, updated weapons, new utility tools, and a more natural healing flow, the game feels more complete, more exciting, and ready for bigger matches.

# Rivals Lite — June 10 Utility Update

## Overview
The June 10 Utility Update expands Rivals Lite with a new offensive utility, a natural healing system, and a clearer loadout structure. These additions are meant to increase tactical depth, reward smart positioning, and make matches feel more dynamic without overcomplicating the core gameplay.

## Design Goals
This update is designed to:
- Add a high-impact utility for pressure and area control
- Give players a more forgiving survival loop through natural healing
- Make loadouts easier to understand while still allowing distinct playstyles

The experience should feel:
- Tactical rather than random
- Rewarding for positioning and timing
- Clear and readable for both new and experienced players

---

## 1. New Utility: Grenade

### Purpose
The grenade is a utility tool for pressure, disruption, and area control. It gives players a way to threaten space, force movement, and punish enemies who overcommit.

### Core Traits
- Deals 50 damage
- Has a 25-second cooldown
- Shows the landing area before impact
- Used for pressure, pushing enemies, and controlling space

### Design Intent
The grenade creates opportunities for:
- Zone denial
- Forced repositioning
- Surprise attacks
- Defensive pressure

### Player Experience
Players should feel that grenades are powerful but require timing and awareness. The visible landing indicator makes the ability readable and gives opponents a chance to react.

---

## 2. Natural Healing System

### Purpose
The healing system makes fights feel more sustainable by rewarding players for staying calm and avoiding repeated damage.

### How It Works
- After taking damage, the player must wait 5 seconds without taking more damage
- Once the delay ends, healing begins
- The player heals 2 HP per second

### Design Intent
This system encourages:
- Defensive spacing
- Recovery after a fight
- Better decision-making during engagements

### Player Experience
Healing should feel natural and fair. It creates a rhythm where players can recover after a burst of damage, but only if they avoid being hit again immediately.

---

## 3. Loadout Update

### New Loadout Rules
Every player always has:
- Primary weapon
- Secondary weapon
- Melee weapon

Then the player chooses one utility:
- Medkit
- Grenade

### Design Intent
This makes the loadout structure simple and consistent while still allowing players to choose a playstyle.

### Loadout Roles
- Medkit: focused on survivability and recovery
- Grenade: focused on pressure, area control, and disruption

### Balance Goal
The loadout system should keep the game readable:
- Primary, secondary, and melee remain the core combat tools
- Utility adds tactical variation without replacing the main weapon loop

---

## 4. Gameplay Flow

### Early Engagement
Players begin with their standard weapons and one chosen utility. The utility can be used to create space, pressure enemies, or survive aggressive fights.

### Mid Fight
The grenade adds control options, while natural healing gives players a path to recover after taking damage. This creates more dynamic combat rather than purely burst-based exchanges.

### Late Fight
The combination of utility pressure and regeneration makes fights more strategic and less dependent on perfect timing alone.

---

## 5. Balance Philosophy
The update should preserve the feel of Rivals Lite while increasing tactical depth.

### Expected Outcomes
- Grenade adds high-value but readable pressure
- Healing keeps fights from feeling too punishing
- Loadout choice creates meaningful differences without overwhelming players

---

## Summary
The June 10 Utility Update introduces:
- A new grenade utility for pressure and area control
- A natural healing system that rewards safe play and recovery
- A streamlined loadout structure with one utility choice

Together, these systems make Rivals Lite more tactical, more readable, and more engaging for both casual and competitive play.
